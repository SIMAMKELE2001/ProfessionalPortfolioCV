About me

Where I Am Now

I am an Advanced Diploma in ICT in Applications Development student and aspiring Software Engineer with a growing focus on backend development, information systems, data, and the engineering of software that solves real problems.

My current journey has been strongly shaped by Java. I have developed a solid foundation in Java, Spring Boot and Python, and I am continuing to deepen my understanding of backend engineering, databases, APIs, software architecture, and the systems that sit behind the applications we use every day.

One experience that has influenced me more than I initially expected is teaching. For the past few years, I have had the opportunity to tutor Java and help other students understand programming concepts, work through problems, and become more confident in their ability to code. Teaching taught me something that textbooks never could: knowing something and being able to help someone else understand it are two very different skills.

It also changed the way I learn. When I teach, I have to understand concepts deeply enough to explain why something works, not simply remember how to do it. That has pushed me to become more curious, analytical, patient, and intentional about my own development as an engineer.

Although backend development is where I feel most at home, I have deliberately explored the other side of software too. I have worked with JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Flutter, and Dart, alongside the fundamentals of web and frontend development. I did this because I wanted to understand more than just what happens inside a server. I wanted to understand how the entire system comes together and, ultimately, how the technology I build is experienced by a real person.

I am still developing as a software engineer, and I am comfortable saying that I have a lot left to learn. For me, that is not a weakness, it is part of the reason I enjoy this field.

Where I'm Going

I want to grow into an engineer who can work on reliable software, meaningful information systems, data-driven solutions, and backend platforms that solve problems beyond the screen.

I am increasingly interested in cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and AI-powered systems, particularly in how these technologies can be combined with good software engineering to build systems that are useful, scalable, and accessible.

But my goal is not simply to learn more technologies or collect frameworks.

I want to understand problems first, systems second, and technology as the tool that connects the two.

I want to contribute to software that makes complicated processes simpler, helps people access information, improves the way organisations work, and creates opportunities where technology can genuinely make a difference. Coming from a background where technology was not always something people around me talked about, I have become particularly interested in what technology can make possible when it is designed around real people's needs.

Outside technology, I am deeply interested in learning and personal growth. Teaching and sharing knowledge is one of the things I enjoy most, not simply because I like explaining concepts, but because I genuinely enjoy seeing someone go from "I don't understand this" to "I can do this."

Reading has become another important part of that journey. It has challenged the way I think, opened me to different ideas, and strengthened my desire to keep learning beyond what is required of me.

I am also intentionally working on myself as a person. I want to become a better communicator, express my ideas more clearly, build confidence, and become more comfortable connecting with people from different backgrounds and perspectives.

So, while I am building myself as a software engineer, I am also building myself as a teacher, communicator, problem solver, and lifelong learner.

And perhaps that is the question that keeps me moving:

What could I build if I combine what I know about technology with what I care about as a person?
